A foundation for a new life

The New Life Program is the Mission’s free, long-term, in-house rehabilitation program for men. It is designed to help participants address challenges that may include addiction, broken relationships, unemployment and other barriers to stability. It is not a short-term detox program.

What participants work on

Through counseling, case management, spiritual development, work readiness and life-skills education, participants develop tools and supportive relationships for lasting change. The program also incorporates accountability, financial management and housing readiness.

How the program begins

Candidates begin at The Crossing in northeast Denver. The program includes orientation, assessment and structured participation, with increasing responsibilities and opportunities as participants progress. Enrollment requirements, eligibility and timelines should be confirmed through the Mission’s current intake guidance.

Connected clinical services: Pathways of Life Recovery Services

Pathways of Life Recovery Services (PLRS) is a Denver Rescue Mission subsidiary supporting the development and delivery of clinical and behavioral health services. DRM has described the transition as bringing clinical services under a dedicated entity to strengthen clinical oversight, expand licensed-staff support and integrate counseling more fully into Mission programs, including New Life. For details about PLRS, visit its website.
