A stable place while rebuilding

Bridge provides transitional housing and case management to individuals and families experiencing homelessness despite having some ongoing income. At The Crossing, participants can focus on building financial stability, strengthening relationships and preparing for their next housing step.

Support beyond a place to stay

Participants have access to case management, educational workshops, life-skills training, financial management guidance and supportive community relationships. Families with children can also connect with youth-focused opportunities.

Program expectations

Bridge is a fee-based transitional program. Applicants must meet income, documentation and other eligibility requirements, and space may be limited. The Mission’s current Get Help page provides detailed qualifications and instructions for contacting staff.

A note about family shelter

Bridge is transitional housing for qualifying participants and families; it is not a same-night emergency family shelter. Families seeking urgent overnight shelter should use the current Get Help information for referrals and other available services.
