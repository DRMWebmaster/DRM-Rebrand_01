Practical help when it matters

Denver Rescue Mission’s Client Services outreach supports families and individuals struggling to afford everyday necessities. The Ministry Outreach Center (MOC) is the primary Denver location for food, clothing, hygiene products and other household assistance.

Food, clothing and hygiene items

Eligible neighbors can access food pantry assistance and clothing and hygiene supplies. The Mission asks guests to present valid photo identification, and limits and availability may apply. The current Get Help page provides distribution hours and any closure notices.

Furniture and household goods

Furniture and some household essentials are made available through approved case managers and partner organizations for qualifying clients. Furniture distribution is not a general public walk-in service; requests follow a separate screening and approval process.

Commodity Assistance Partners (CAPs)

The Mission partners with pre-approved nonprofit organizations through its Commodity Assistance Partner program, helping those partners obtain eligible clothing, food and hygiene items for their work. The Mission’s current service information states that it is not accepting new CAP applications. Approved partners should use the current instructions for scheduling pickups.

Ministry Outreach Center

5725 East 39th Avenue, Denver, CO 80207. This is the primary site for Client Services distributions and the Mission’s donation drop-offs. Service distribution hours and donation drop-off hours are not necessarily the same.

How to help

You can support these services by giving food, clothing, hygiene items and accepted household goods. Before dropping off donations, review the current Give Items page for accepted items and drop-off information.
