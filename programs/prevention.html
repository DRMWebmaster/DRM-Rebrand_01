Programs

Prevention

Help families stay in their homes

In partnership with public schools, Denver Rescue Mission’s Prevention program helps families at risk of losing their housing with one-time financial assistance and case management.

Families and individuals with low incomes can face difficult choices between rent and other bills. This program works to stop homelessness before it starts.

Prevention as a strategy

Prevention is also one of the Mission’s five connected strategies. The Prevention program is one way that strategy is put into practice.

Read about the Prevention strategy.

Looking for help?

The Ministry Outreach Center also provides food, clothing, hygiene items and household essentials for people experiencing poverty or at risk of homelessness. Find service details and next steps.
