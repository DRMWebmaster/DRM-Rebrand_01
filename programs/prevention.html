Stopping homelessness before it starts

Denver Rescue Mission’s Prevention program supports families with children who are housed but facing an unexpected financial crisis. Working in partnership with public schools, the Mission connects families with trained case managers and short-term assistance intended to prevent a first episode of homelessness.

How Prevention works

School staff identify and refer families experiencing housing instability. Mission case managers work directly with participating families to understand the immediate need, provide individualized support, and connect them with resources such as budgeting and employment-related assistance. One-time financial support may be part of an approved plan.

Our school partnerships

The program launched in October 2025 with Jefferson County Public Schools and Poudre School District. Families are connected through participating school partners rather than a general walk-in application process.

Prevention is a program and a strategy

Prevention is also one of the Mission’s five core strategies. This page describes the specific school-partnership program; the What We Do page explains how Prevention connects with Dignity, Recovery, Readiness and Housing.
