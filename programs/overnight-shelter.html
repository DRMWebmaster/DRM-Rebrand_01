Meeting urgent needs

The Mission operates emergency shelter services for men in Denver. Overnight beds, meals and essential amenities provide a safer place to begin addressing immediate needs and connecting with staff.

Where shelter services are offered

Lawrence Street Shelter and the 48th Avenue Center support emergency shelter in Denver. The Lawrence Street Community Center serves as a central point for guest intake, bed assignments and connections to other providers. The 48th Avenue Center operates in partnership with the City of Denver.

Daytime services and meals

The Lawrence Street Community Center offers guests a place to access meals, water, restrooms and supportive connections during designated hours. Daytime access and meal eligibility can vary for shelter guests and others seeking assistance; confirm the latest information before visiting.

Coordinated access and support

Emergency shelter staff help guests connect with case management, additional resources and longer-term Mission programs where appropriate. Current bed availability, hours and intake procedures are maintained on the Mission’s Get Help page.
