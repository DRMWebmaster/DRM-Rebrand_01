# Pathways of Life Recovery Services content review

## Verified sources

- [PLRS home](https://pathwaysofliferecovery.com/)
- [PLRS frequently asked questions](https://pathwaysofliferecovery.com/faq/)

PLRS identifies itself as Pathways of Life Recovery Services, LLC, a subsidiary of Denver Rescue Mission. Its clinical outpatient treatment complements DRM’s broader recovery support. The draft explains this connection without presenting PLRS treatment as automatic enrollment in a Mission program or promising a particular outcome.
