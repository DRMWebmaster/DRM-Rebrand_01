Ways To Give

Give A Vehicle

Donate your car, truck, motorcycle, or other vehicle today!

When you donate a car, we’ll fix it up and either give it directly to program graduates or sell it to support Mission programs and services. For many people, owning a vehicle is a huge step toward independence.

Furthermore, it can save time and money for individuals and families coming out of poverty and homelessness, and provide stability and increased opportunities.

Get Started Now

Need help or have questions? Call us at 303.331.2938

What Type of Car Can I Donate?

We accept vehicles in any condition. Cars, trucks or other donated vehicles that need moderate repairs are ideal, but we also accept cars that are not running and even some vehicles you may think are “junk.”

Cars that cannot be repaired and/or gifted for any reason can still be used to support Mission programs and services, so please contact us with any questions.

Still wonder if your vehicle is good to donate? Don’t worry. Contact us today at 303.331.2938 to see how you can donate your car.

Benefits of Donating a Car

When you donate your car to Denver Rescue Mission, you’re providing independence and transportation to someone who has worked hard for months in one of our programs to overcome homelessness and poverty. For someone who has lost everything, your car could be the difference that helps them be able to keep their new job, care for their children, make it on time to necessary appointments, and more.

And you’ll benefit too! All donated vehicles, regardless of condition, are tax-deductible. So instead of junking a car for a couple hundred dollars, donate your vehicle to receive more value for your tax-deductible gift.

Donate a Car Today

It’s easy, free and tax deductible. Every vehicle donation situation is unique, but the best way to get started is to contact us. Start by sending us some details about your vehicle, and we’ll get in touch with you to answer any questions.

Your Vehicle Can Change Lives!

Many people would break down in tears when they receive their vehicle.”Mike “My daughter and I are so grateful to have a reliable vehicle. We are members of a church and it is important to us to be active members but we were limited by transportation. Even getting to family events presented its challenges. It’s not easy asking people for a ride or limiting the time you can spend in one place because of public transportation. If I could convey one thing to the vehicle donors its, your donation makes a difference.”Barry “Writing the Thank You card to the donor was a great part of receiving my vehicle. It was so nice to be able to tell that person what their donation was going to do for our lives. It was approaching winter season and I remember being so overjoyed that my son and I wouldn’t be at the bus stop in the cold. I was also thrilled that I no longer needed to plan for a two hour commute as well.”Donna
