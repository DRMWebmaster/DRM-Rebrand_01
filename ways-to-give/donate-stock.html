Benefits of Donating Stock

Donating stock is fast, safe, and easy to do. Denver Rescue Mission has partnered with LPL Financial to enable our supporters to make stock donations quick and hassle-free. Simply share this information with your broker and ask them to transfer any appreciated stocks you want to donate directly into our brokerage account.  

Did you know that when you donate stock, you can avoid the tax on the gain while deducting 100% of the value of your contribution? When the market is up, it’s the perfect time to lock in 2-3x the tax savings and the ability to give even more vs. donating cash. It’s a win-win for you and us, and for our homeless neighbors whose lives are change from your kindness.

Tax Benefits You May Receive

  • No capital gain to be recognized
  • Full value of stock is tax deductible
  • May qualify for Homeless Contribution Tax Credit
  • Stocks, Bonds and most Mutual Funds are accepted

Please note: We may not be able to accept some mutual funds directly into our account. If you wish to make a gift of mutual funds, please contact us.

Have questions? Please contact us or call 303.297.1008 for assistance.

Why Support Denver Rescue Mission?

Ending Homelessness One Life at a Time

Every day, we help end homelessness in the lives of men, women and children in our city. Thanks to the support of partners like you, thousands of people experience Christ’s love at what is probably the most desperate point in their lives.

Guests Experience Foundation-First Growth

While “housing first” is popular, we believe in a “foundation-first” approach. By addressing the root causes of homelessness, we help our guests build stability and life skills, setting them up for long-term success.

Our Programs and Services Address a Variety of Needs

The solutions to homelessness are often just as complex as it’s causes. Our programs and services are designed to meet each of our guests at their unique point of need, whether that’s emergency services, rehabilitation, programs to help working families transition into housing, or community services like a food pantry that help people stay in housing by offsetting the cost of essentials.

Your Gift Goes Further

At Denver Rescue Mission, your gift goes further. We leverage our resources, partnerships, and experience to provide comprehensive care and solutions that create lasting change.

Does Your Stock Donation Qualify for Additional Tax Credit Benefits?

Homeless Contribution Tax Credits (HCTC) allow a dollar-for-dollar reduction in state taxes owed by an amount equal to 25% of stock donations to Denver Rescue Mission of $250 or more.

If your gift qualifies, you have to request the tax-credit in order to receive it, so be sure to notify us of your stock donation gift if you are interested in HCTC.

Please remember to always consult a tax professional for how your donations may impact your taxes.

Is the Mission In Your Will or Estate Plan?

By joining our Goodheart Society with a gift in your Will, you make a lasting impact through your estate plans or life-income gifts to Denver Rescue Mission. Named after Jim Goodheart, a former guest and Mission Director, your membership grants you exclusive event invitations, volunteer opportunities, and special updates from our leadership.
