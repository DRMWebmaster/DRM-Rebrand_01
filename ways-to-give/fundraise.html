Fundraise for Denver Rescue Mission

Working to end homelessness, one life at a time — and you can be part of it.

When you start a peer-to-peer fundraiser for Denver Rescue Mission, you invite your friends, family, and community to help change lives across the Denver metro area. The funds you raise help provide safe shelter, nourishing meals, recovery programs, and personalized support that empowers individuals and families to move toward lasting stability. Your support also helps us step in early — providing critical assistance that can prevent families from ever experiencing homelessness in the first place.

It’s easy to get started: create your page, share why this mission matters to you, and invite others to give. Together, we can reach neighbors in need and move closer to ending homelessness, one life at a time.
