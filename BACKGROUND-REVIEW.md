# Source background review

Reviewed 45 available source URLs, including the navigation pages, homepage, source overviews, external Blog and donation destinations, and supporting location pages. The unavailable internships sources and pages without a direct live equivalent retain their existing draft treatments. External checkout styling and location-detail callouts are not placed into unrelated GitHub sections.

## What was restored

- 84 source-colored or photographic content sections across the draft, plus 22 matching program-photo cards.
- 29 background image files stored locally, with original URLs and checksums in `assets/backgrounds/sources.json`.
- White source-aligned headers on 13 pages with text-only or empty source banners, with Blueberry text for readability.
- Original section colors, including Oatmeal panels, Water partnership sections, Blueberry resource panels and DRM Red action sections.
- Background photos remain with the corresponding section content and use the observed source crop positions. A readable overlay is retained for photographic text panels. Fixed/parallax scrolling is not reproduced, so mobile scrolling stays predictable.
- The current homepage hero photo, impact and story backgrounds, action color and latest-news section color are restored, including the four published homepage impact figures. The story and news sections include the four authentic quotes and four article previews shown on the source homepage.
- The homepage’s supporting sections follow the source’s impact → stories → action → program support → latest-news order, after the retained five-strategy introduction.
- Program-group photos appear on the matching program previews. The obsolete four-strategy categorization and its category color coding are not restored; GitHub’s five-strategy framework remains authoritative.

The responsive navigation, local search, accessible accordions, verified service content and all Formstack placeholders remain in place.
