## Review notes

- Formstack script embeds are replaced with blocks labeled **Formstack Form Here**. Forms are not submitted from these placeholders.
- Service schedules, financial figures, reports, staff names and eligibility rules come from the live sources. Linked reports remain at their original published locations.
- Get Help keeps emergency guidance in English and Spanish, shelter referrals for women and children, intake instructions, recovery and Bridge requirements, and essentials/partner access details. Expired June inventory and undated extreme-weather alerts are excluded.
- The live Get Help page gives differing descriptions of Community Center access and an unclear New Life Program timeline. Those differences are disclosed with direct contact information rather than silently reconciled. The Northern Colorado shelter link points to the location’s current closure/update information.
- The current Thanksgiving notice is retained; check registration availability before sharing it.
- Both /internships/ and /get-involved/internships/ returned 404. The internship page is based on the visible Careers information and directs readers to current opportunities.
- PLRS is Pathways of Life Recovery Services. Its page now uses the official PLRS home, FAQ and contact pages; see `PLRS-REVIEW.md` for the verified relationship, services and remaining source discrepancies.
- The Who We Are overview has no substantive source body. Its overview is composed from the live mission, vision, history, leadership and reporting pages. Other Ways to Give is compiled from the live giving overview.
- Program pages without direct live equivalents use the relevant Get Help and How We Help material. Synthesized introductions and navigation are distinguished from direct carryovers in the table.
- Imagined program stories, provisional relationships, prototype-only FAQs and invented testimonials have been removed. Core strategy explanations from GitHub are retained.
- Blog and Give Now remain external navigation destinations. No new donation processing or subscription integration is introduced.
