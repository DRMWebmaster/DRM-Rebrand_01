Be the Change in our Community

“It’s an incredible opportunity to make a meaningful difference in the lives of those in need. Your involvement is a chance to connect with others, share in the spirit of giving, and see firsthand the impact of your generosity—you can help create positive change in our community.”

Every day of every year, Denver Rescue Mission provides meals, safe shelter, life-changing programs, and the resources to help individuals and families rebuild their lives. Just as powerful as the services that are offered, the Mission is a place where people are treated with dignity, compassion and opportunities for lasting change.

This work is only possible because of the faithful support of our community. Sponsorships play a vital role, and we invite you to be a part of true transformation for this community.

Why Sponsor?

Our goal is to end homelessness, one life at a time, and your sponsorship helps make taking this step possible for thousands of guests each year. Denver Rescue Mission is committed to meeting the needs in our community through emergency services, recovery programs, transitional programs, and homelessness prevention. These services are life-changing opportunities to help individuals and families restore hope and achieve self-sufficiency.

Your partnership with us is part of a bigger picture of support in our community through:

IMPACTFUL COMMUNITY CHANGE

By joining with us, your company becomes part of an enduring legacy of hope that has empowered thousands of men, women and children to overcome homelessness and addiction.

BRAND ALIGNMENT

Show your company’s commitment to corporate social responsibility that fosters trust within the community.

HIGH-VALUE VISIBILITY

Gain exposure through events, media coverage and ongoing recognition to other Mission supporters throughout the year.

TEAM ENGAGEMENT

Offer meaningful volunteer opportunities that inspire your employees.

“At First Integrity Title, we believe that caring for the hurting, the lost, and the broken is our joint responsibility, and we are proud to partner with the Denver Rescue Mission and all of the amazing work they do in our fantastic state of Colorado.”

Sponsorship Opportunities & Giving Levels

Partnering with Denver Rescue Mission highlights your company’s commitment to helping individuals and families move from homelessness into stable housing. Your sponsorship supports critical programs that provide shelter, case management and pathways to permanent housing and employment.

In return, your business gains meaningful visibility, employee volunteer opportunities and recognition as a leader in corporate social responsibility. Together, we can rehouse our neighbors in need and build a stronger, more resilient Denver.
