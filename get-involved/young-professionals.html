Serve, Learn, Connect

Denver Rescue Mission’s Young Professionals group is seeking passionate individuals to join its growing network of influencers. As the next generation of leaders, our goal is to help people who are experiencing homelessness and addiction in our city change their lives.

We strive to make an impact through hands-on volunteering, learning about current issues affecting homelessness, engaging in events and networking, and connecting with our greater Denver community. Come serve, learn and connect with us!

Serve

  • Join us for a recurring opportunity to prepare and serve meals to people in need
  • Meet the needs of the men, women, and children served by Denver Rescue Mission in other diverse ways
  • Sustain the life-changing work happening throughout the Mission 

Learn

  • Hear from experts on homelessness-related topics
  • Gain a greater understanding of how to make a difference

Connect

  • Network with other young professionals in the Denver area
  • Become a Denver Rescue Mission ambassador and spark change with others in your community 

Sign Up to Join Us Today!

Signing up is simple, and we’ll instantly get you connected to other young professionals that are committed to serving people experiencing homelessness in Denver. We offer two levels of engagement, so select the type of membership that interests you below to get started.

Impact Members

Impact members have a recurring monthly gift, enjoying all the benefits of social members as well as other exclusive opportunities.

Become an Impact Member

Social Members

Social members are invited to volunteering, social and fundraising opportunities throughout the year, but miss out on special events reserved for Impact members.
