Frequently Asked Questions

What does Denver Rescue Mission do?

Denver Rescue Mission meets the needs of those experiencing poverty and homelessness through emergency services, rehabilitation programs, transitional programs, and community outreach. Since 1892, the Mission has been changing lives in our community.

If you or a loved one needs help, click Get Help or call 303.297.1815.

Where is Denver Rescue Mission located?

We have five different locations throughout Denver and two in northern Colorado. Each location serves a unique and vital role in helping address the issues related to homelessness and in helping those experiencing homelessness return to society as productive, self-sufficient citizens.

You can learn more about our locations here.

How do I volunteer at Denver Rescue Mission?

Volunteers are the life-blood of Denver Rescue Mission. Short-term and long-term opportunities are available, including serving meals, sorting clothing, becoming a mentor, and more!

Becoming a volunteer is easy. Simply visit our Volunteer page, create a volunteer profile and choose a serving shift and facility that works with your schedule. (We do ask that you sign up to volunteer on our website and not show up unannounced.)

For more information, call our volunteer hotline at 303.953.3955.

Can I donate online?

Monetary donations can be made online, by mail or by phone.

Does Denver Rescue Mission participate in any door-to-door solicitation?

Denver Rescue Mission does not support, partner or benefit from any individuals or companies claiming to raise funds through door-to-door solicitations.

If you have any questions of our involvement in a fundraising program, please contact us at 303.297.1815.

What types of gift-in-kind donations (items) do you accept?

The Mission always needs non-perishable food, clothing, hygiene items, furniture, and household items.

Learn More

Do you pick up donations?

We offer free pickups for furniture and large items. We do not pick up clothing, food or household items unless they are in addition to a piece of furniture or a large item. Learn more about scheduling a furniture pickup here.

What is Denver Rescue Mission’s Tax I.D. #?

The Mission’s tax I.D. number is 84-6038762.

Does Denver Rescue Mission provide rental assistance?

The Mission’s Bridge Program provides transitional housing at The Crossing, and our Pathway Home program provides assistance for permanent housing as well as community mentoring and connection. These are the only transitional and assistance programs available. Learn how to Get Help here.

Does Denver Rescue Mission provide assistance with utilities?

No. Please contact your local city/county for assistance programs.

How can I receive assistance with food and clothing?

The Client Services department at our Ministry Outreach Center provides a wide range of  community outreach services including a food pantry and clothing while also offering emotional and spiritual support.

How will I know my donation was completed?

When you make an online donation, you will receive an email confirmation message once your donation is complete. Receipts will also be mailed to the address you have provided within 7-10 business days for gifts $5 or greater. Login to access your donor account.

My credit card was rejected. What should I do?

We use a secure payment processor to ensure that your credit card information is safe. Sometimes the payment processor may reject your information for unspecified reasons. If the charge has been blocked on your bank statement, we recommend contacting your issuing bank to understand why the charge was blocked.

You can also call us at 303.297.1815 to donate securely over the phone.

Is my online donation processed securely?

Protecting your online security when you make a donation is our top priority.

Denver Rescue Mission uses industry-leading Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) technology to keep your personal information as secure as possible and protect against the loss, misuse and alteration of the information you entrust to us.

We protect your information by working with partners that provide a secure and safe environment for credit card donations. All information is stored in a secure database. For more information, view our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Is my donation tax deductible?​

Donations made to Denver Rescue Mission are 100% tax deductible to the full extent permitted by law.

The Mission is a registered 501(c)3 charitable organization (EIN: 84-6038762). We issue tax receipts at the time of donation. If you would like to request a receipt for one or more donations you made this year, please contact us or access your online donor account.

Some Mission events may include a registration fee, which is often either not tax deductible, or only partially tax deductible.

Please consult with your tax advisor for proper tax treatment of any donations

How can I change or update my credit card information?

If you are a monthly donor, you can update your donation credit card details on our donor login portal.

If you would like to change or update your credit card information over the phone, please call 303.297.1815 and we will be happy to assist you.

If you do not have a recurring gift set up, our third-party partners do not retain your credit card details. Denver Rescue Mission does not retain credit card numbers for any online donations. Recurring donations are processed by a trusted third-party vendor who meets Level 1 PCI compliance standards. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

How can I change or cancel my donation?

You can make adjustments to your donation by calling us at 303.297.1815. Monthly donors can adjust their giving plans on our donor account portal.

Where does my donation go?

When you give to Denver Rescue Mission, you want to know that your gifts are really making a difference. That’s why we’re committed to helping you understand the real impact of your donations and how your gifts help change lives. See our Financial Accountability page for more details on how your donation has made an impact in our community.

How can I change my mailing address?​

To change your mailing address, please call 303.297.1815 or contact us online.

How can I update my mailing preferences?​

Our mailings are one of the ways we report back to our donors about the lives changed because of their support, and they are an important way we receive gifts from our supporters. Often, these mailers serve as a reminder that we need our supporters’ help throughout the year and, without them, we would be unable to continue providing food, shelter and life-changing programs to people in need.

If you want to reduce or change the number of mailings you receive, we’re happy to help. Simply call us at 303.297.1815 or contact us online to update your mail preferences. Any change will take 8-12 weeks to take effect.
