Board of Directors

Denver Rescue Mission’s Board of Directors is comprised of dedicated individuals who are committed to the mission of providing hope, healing, and restoration to those experiencing homelessness and poverty in the Denver metro area. Our board members are passionate about making a positive impact on our community and ensuring that the Mission operates with integrity and efficiency.
