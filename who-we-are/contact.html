Contacting a Guest or Program Participant+

While we understand the desire to contact a friend or loved one who may be receiving services or participating in a program at the Mission, it is our policy to not disclose any information about our guests or program participants. We can neither confirm nor deny that an individual has been or is a guest or program participant at one of our facilities.
